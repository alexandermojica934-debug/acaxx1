## 11. Verification Addendum — What Is Real, What Requires Validation

### A. Unclaimed-property recovery is regulated work, not an automatic revenue stream
- The earlier pipeline totals, company names, asset amounts, and estimated fees shown in the NEXUS mockup are **unverified until each record is found in the applicable official state database and matched to the actual legal owner**. Treat them as leads only, not recoverable assets or earned revenue.
- In Florida, a paid claimant representative must be a Florida-licensed private investigator, Florida-certified public accountant, or Florida-licensed attorney **and** hold Department registration. A person cannot lawfully file paid recovery claims as a representative merely by using a private locator agreement. [Florida Stat. §717.1400](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0700-0799/0717/Sections/0717.1400.html)
- Florida requires the Department’s prescribed **Abandoned Property Recovery Agreement** or **Abandoned Property Purchase Agreement** as the exclusive contract form. A custom fee/locator agreement for Florida-held property is void. Fees and costs are capped at 30% and are payable only after an approved claim. [Florida Stat. §717.135](https://www.leg.state.fl.us/statutes/index.cfm?App_mode=Display_Statute&Search_String=&URL=0700-0799/0717/Sections/0717.135.html)
- The State of Florida’s free process allows owners to claim their property directly. Third-party outreach must never imply government affiliation, urgency, guaranteed recovery, or that a fee is required. [Florida Division of Unclaimed Property](https://www.fltreasurehunt.gov/UP-Web/sitePages/Received-Contacted.jsp)

### B. DeFi liquidation monitoring can be built at zero cost; executing liquidations cannot reliably be zero-capital
- Aave and Morpho liquidations require the liquidator to repay borrower debt, then receive collateral plus a liquidation bonus. Gas, slippage, execution competition, and sufficient liquidity all affect profitability. [Aave v3](https://aave.com/docs/aave-v3/overview) [Morpho liquidation documentation](https://docs.morpho.org/learn/concepts/liquidation/)
- The viable no-capital proof of concept is **monitoring and simulation**: track candidate positions, calculate net profitability after estimated costs, timestamp the alert, and compare it with the outcome. Any later automated execution requires capital or a carefully audited flash-loan transaction and should be independently security-reviewed.
